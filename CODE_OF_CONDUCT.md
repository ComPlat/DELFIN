# Code of Conduct

DELFIN is used and built by researchers across several groups. This is the
short version of what that requires of everyone taking part.

## What is expected

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
Assume the other side is trying to get the science right, because they almost
always are.

Criticism of a method, a result or a piece of code is welcome and necessary —
a wrong number that nobody challenges ends up in a paper. Direct that at the
work: "this comparison excludes the unconverged states, so the preferred value
is not what it looks like" rather than anything about who wrote it.

Say when you do not know. A question asked early costs less than a calculation
run for three days on a wrong assumption.

## What is not

Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption. Publishing someone's
private information. Deliberately misrepresenting someone's work or results.

## If something goes wrong

Raise it with the maintainers at the ComPlat group, KIT. Reports are handled
privately, and whoever reports something is not put at a disadvantage for
having done so.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts where that is
needed to keep the project usable for everybody else.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and wherever somebody is representing the project.
